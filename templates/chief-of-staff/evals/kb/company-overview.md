# StockSync — company overview

> Fictional company used only by the agy-hq eval suite (templates/chief-of-staff/evals).
> It exists so eval results never depend on a real company's knowledge base.

StockSync is an inventory and order synchronisation service for online
retailers in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. A seller connects every sales channel
once, and StockSync keeps stock levels identical across all of them.

- Headquarters: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- Languages: Vietnamese and English.
- Team: a Sales SDR agent (Mai) for new leads, an Account Manager agent (Linh)
  for existing customers, and human owners for pricing, billing, legal and
  engineering escalations.
