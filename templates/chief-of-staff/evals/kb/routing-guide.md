# StockSync — routing guide

> Fictional company used only by the agy-hq eval suite (templates/chief-of-staff/evals).

- Company language for internal digests: Vietnamese. Address the owner as "anh/chị".
- Human owners for escalations: Ms. Thu (sales, contracts, legal, data
  protection, press), Mr. Duc (billing, refunds, invoices), the engineering
  on-call (outages and bugs).
- Urgent for the owner: anything legal, security, angry or threatening, and any
  money dispute.
- Known noise: newsletters from marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada) and vendor
  promotions can be ignored.
