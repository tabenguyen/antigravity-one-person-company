# StockSync — ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Good fit

- Online retailers selling physical products on two or more channels, for
  example Shopee + Lazada, or a marketplace + their own Shopify/WooCommerce site.
- 5 to 200 employees (or roughly 100+ orders a month across channels).
- Based in Vietnam or elsewhere in Southeast Asia.
- Contact is the owner, e-commerce manager, head of operations or similar.
- Pain: overselling, stockouts on one channel while another still shows stock,
  manual end-of-day inventory reconciliation.

## Not a fit (disqualify, do not BANT-score)

- Businesses that do not sell physical products online: law firms, consultancies,
  restaurants, agencies, SaaS companies, schools, and similar services.
- Sellers on only one channel with no plan to add another.
- Individuals and companies with fewer than 3 employees.
- Companies outside Southeast Asia.
- Resellers or competitors of inventory-sync software.
