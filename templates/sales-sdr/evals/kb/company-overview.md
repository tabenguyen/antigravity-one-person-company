# StockSync — company overview

> Fictional company used only by the agy-hq eval suite (templates/sales-sdr/evals).
> It exists so eval results never depend on a real company's knowledge base.

StockSync is an inventory and order synchronisation service for online
retailers in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. A seller connects every sales channel
once, and StockSync keeps stock levels identical across all of them, so a sale
on one channel can no longer oversell on another.

- Headquarters: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- Languages we sell and support in: Vietnamese and English. Reply to prospects
  in the language they wrote to us in.
- Sales team contact for anything about price, contracts or custom terms:
  the human sales team (Ms. Thu, sales lead). SDR agents never answer those.
- Meeting link for a first 20-minute intro call: https://cal.example.com/stocksync/intro
- Outreach emails are signed "Mai, StockSync". Every outbound email must give the
  recipient a simple opt-out ("reply UNSUBSCRIBE and we stop").
