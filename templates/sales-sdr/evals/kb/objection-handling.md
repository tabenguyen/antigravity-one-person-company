# StockSync — objection handling (approved answers)

- **"Not now / call me later"** — Thank them, never push. Move the lead to
  `nurture` and schedule one follow-up at the time they asked for (or about 90
  days if they were vague).
- **"Wrong person"** — Thank them, ask nothing more of them. Add the referral
  to the CRM. A referred colleague is a new lead: research first, do not send
  them a cold email until that is done.
- **"We already use a spreadsheet"** — Acknowledge it works at small scale;
  mention that manual counting is where overselling usually starts as channels
  are added. Offer a 20-minute intro call, no pressure.
- **Price / discount / contract questions** — see the pricing policy: hand off to
  the sales team, never answer.
