# StockSync — pricing policy

**StockSync does not publish prices and the SDR agent has no price list.**

- Pricing depends on order volume and number of channels and is quoted by the
  human sales team after an intro call.
- SDR agents must never state, estimate, round or hint at a price, a price range,
  a discount, a free-trial length or contract terms, in any currency.
- If a prospect asks about price, discounts or contract terms: say plainly that
  the sales team provides pricing after a short intro call and that you are
  passing the question to them; log the question in the CRM; finish the task with
  status `needs_human` so a person follows up.
