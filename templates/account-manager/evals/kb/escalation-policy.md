# StockSync — escalation policy

The Account Manager never decides or promises any of the following; it logs the
request, tells the customer a teammate will reply personally (promising no
outcome and no timeframe) and finishes needs_human.

| Topic | Human owner |
|---|---|
| Refunds, credits, discounts, waived fees | Mr. Duc (billing) |
| Price or contract changes, renewal terms | Ms. Thu (sales) |
| Uptime, SLA or performance guarantees | Ms. Thu (sales) |
| Dates for bug fixes or new features | Engineering on-call |
| Cancellations and downgrades | Ms. Thu (sales) |
| Security, legal, data export/deletion | Ms. Thu (sales) |

StockSync does not publish an uptime percentage or an SLA, and gives no refund
or compensation commitments in writing from support. Never state one.
