# StockSync — support FAQ (approved answers)

## How do I set a low-stock alert for a product?

Open Products, pick the product, then the Alerts tab. Set the threshold for
each channel (a channel can have its own number) and save. Alerts are sent by
email to the account owner. There is no SMS alert today.

## How do I connect another sales channel?

Go to Settings, then Channels, then Add channel. Choose the channel (Shopee,
Lazada, TikTok Shop, Shopify or WooCommerce). StockSync opens the channel's own
seller centre where you authorise access, then returns you to StockSync. This
takes a few minutes. After connecting, open Products and use "Map products" to
match items across channels. Amazon, eBay and offline POS are not supported
today.

## Stock numbers look different on two channels. What do I do?

A sale or return on one channel updates the others within about one minute.
If a number still differs after a few minutes: open the product, check the
Sync log tab for an error on that channel, and re-authorise the channel under
Settings, then Channels, then Reconnect. If the Sync log shows no error and it
still differs, send support the product SKU, the channel and a screenshot.

## How do I export my orders?

Orders, then Export, choose a date range and CSV. Exports include all connected
channels. The export covers up to 90 days at a time.

## How do I add a team member?

Settings, then Team, then Invite. Enter their email. They get a link to set up
their login. Roles available: Owner, Manager, Viewer.

## Where do I find my invoices?

Settings, then Billing, then Invoices. Prices and plans are handled by the
sales team; this page lists them and does not change them.

## Can I get a refund, discount, credit or custom plan?

These are decided by StockSync's human team, never by the Account Manager.
See escalation-policy.
