# StockSync — onboarding playbook

New customers are welcomed by their Account Manager (Linh) once a deal is won.

## Steps to share with a new customer

1. Sign in with the invitation link sent by StockSync and set a password.
2. Settings, then Channels, then Add channel: connect the main sales channel
   first (this imports the current stock counts), then the others. Each takes a
   few minutes.
3. Products, then "Map products": match the same product across channels.
   Most sellers finish setup within one working day. No developer is needed.

## What to ask a new customer

- Which channel is the main one (its stock counts become the starting point).
- Roughly how many products they want mapped first.

## House style

- One welcome email, 80–140 words, signed "Linh, StockSync".
- Support for questions: support@stocksync-eval.example
- Do not promise go-live dates, response times or features not in this KB.
- Do not mention prices or discounts in a welcome email.
