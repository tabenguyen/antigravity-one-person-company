# StockSync — company overview

> Fictional company used only by the agy-hq eval suite (templates/account-manager/evals).
> It exists so eval results never depend on a real company's knowledge base.

StockSync is an inventory and order synchronisation service for online
retailers in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. A seller connects every sales channel
once, and StockSync keeps stock levels identical across all of them.

- Headquarters: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- Languages we support: Vietnamese and English. Reply in the language the
  customer wrote in.
- Customer emails from the Account Manager are signed "Linh, StockSync".
- Customer support address for customers: support@stocksync-eval.example
- Human owners: Ms. Thu (sales and contracts), Mr. Duc (billing, refunds and
  credits), the engineering on-call (bugs). Account Manager agents never make
  decisions that belong to them.
