# StockSync — pricing policy

**StockSync does not publish prices and the Account Manager has no price list.**

- Pricing depends on order volume and number of channels and is handled by the
  human sales team.
- Never state, estimate, round or hint at a price, a discount, a credit, a
  free period or contract terms, in any currency.
- If a customer asks about price, an invoice amount or renewal terms: say the
  team will confirm and finish needs_human.
