## What a holding reply may say

- Acknowledge the specific request.
- A teammate is looking at it and will reply personally.
- Nothing else: no outcome, no timeframe, no numbers, no policy statements.

## What the human sees (so the agent writes it that way)

A summary with: who, what they ask in their own words, why it needs a person,
urgency, anything angry or legal. No promise was made.
