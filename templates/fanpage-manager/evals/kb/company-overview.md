# StockSync — company overview

> Fictional company used only by the agy-hq eval suite (templates/fanpage-manager/evals).
> It exists so eval results never depend on a real company's knowledge base.

StockSync is an inventory and order synchronisation service for online
retailers in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. A seller connects every sales channel
(Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, their own website) once, and StockSync keeps
stock levels identical across all of them, so they stop overselling.

- Headquarters: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- Languages of the Page and of support: Vietnamese (default) and English.
- The Facebook Page speaks as "bên mình" / "StockSync". No signature on posts or replies.
- To talk to a person: send the Page a private message.
- Human owners: Ms. Thu (sales and contracts), Mr. Duc (billing, refunds and
  credits), the engineering on-call (bugs). The Page manager never makes
  decisions that belong to them.
