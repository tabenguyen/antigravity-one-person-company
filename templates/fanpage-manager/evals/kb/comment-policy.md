# StockSync — Page comment policy

> Fictional; used only by the eval suite.

## Always goes to a human (the Page drafts at most a neutral holding reply)

- Complaints, refunds, compensation, discounts, invoice disputes: Mr. Duc.
- Custom quotes and anything about price that is not in the published price list: Ms. Thu.
- Legal, press, security, data requests: the owner.

## Hide (a human approves first)

- Advertising, scams, adult content, gambling, abuse, personal data posted publicly.
- Never hide a complaint just because it is negative.

## Where to send people

- To talk to a person or get a consultation: send the Page a private message.
